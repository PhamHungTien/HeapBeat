HEAPBEAT PIANO AUDIO NOTICE
Version 1.0 - July 2026

HeapBeat bundles 18 piano recordings supplied by the project owner for an
internal, non-commercial course demonstration. The application preserves the
performer attribution found in each MP3 file:

- Cà Pháo Pianist
- Người Nghiện Chơi Đàn
- Vũ Ngọc Tiến
- Yuriko Piano

The recordings are piano covers. Possession of an audio file does not by itself
grant public-performance rights in the underlying composition. Before using
HeapBeat outside the classroom or on a public speaker system, the deployer must
verify the recording license, composition rights, attribution requirements, and
any applicable public-performance license for every track.

HeapBeat does not download audio from Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, or a remote
streaming API. Playback uses only the local MP3 files in public/music through
the browser's native HTMLAudioElement.

Project: HeapBeat - Community Music Queue for Study Rooms
